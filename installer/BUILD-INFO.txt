PACKAGING NOTE:
---------------
forge.config.js now sets packagerConfig.ignore. Without it Packager copies
the whole project directory into app.asar, which previously bundled .env
(real secrets), the prior installer/ build, and a stray 104 MB
SoundBoard-Setup.exe. That is why the earlier build was 890 MB and a
rebuild reached 1413 MB. Keep the ignore list current when adding new
top-level folders.
